Why Yea I Consulting?

What 20 years of agency client service taught me about how companies work, and how they can adapt to AI.

Most AI consultants are either technologists who know the tools but struggle to speak the language of business, or generalists who've read the right reports but haven't spent years inside businesses with tight budgets and staff to keep on side.

"Agency life teaches you to learn how a client's business works fast, because you're no use to their marketing until you do, and AI is the same. I've also spent 20 years around a fair amount of bullshit, which turns out to be good training for the AI market, so I start with how your business runs and who does what, and only then look at which tools might help."

Straight talk

If AI isn't the right answer for something, I'll tell you, and I'll tell you why.

20 years with multinational brands

I've worked with The Coca-Cola Company, Nestlé, the BBC and Pokémon, and agency client service is mostly learning how someone else's business works, which is most of what AI strategy needs.

You talk to the person doing the work

I take every call, write every report and lead every workshop myself, and the only help I have is with admin, so you'll deal with me throughout.

AI shouldn't cost anyone their job

I won't help a business make someone redundant so that AI can do their job. Done right, AI lets the people you already have take on more work, and that's the version I help build.
